Detailed Description

Description of a minor compaction job (some stores turned into one).

Tombstones are pruned based on the origins of the stores that aren't included (see IHost::StoreInfo and Compaction::GetStoreOrigins()). The "oldest store id" isn't used for this, as stores made by compactions get new ids, so a store with a newer id than a tombstone's could still have an older version of the deleted item.
